Shorts Publisher

Shorts Publisher is a private publishing tool. The channel owner uses it to upload short videos they prepared to their own YouTube channels on a schedule.

There is no public sign-up and no third-party users. Only the owner of a channel connects it, through the standard Google sign-in screen.

The tool uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, read the connected channel's name, and update or remove videos it uploaded earlier.
